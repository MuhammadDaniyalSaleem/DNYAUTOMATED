FAQ

Before we build.

What can DNYAUTOMATED automate?

Repetitive, rules-based or data-heavy processes across sales, operations, documents, reporting, integrations and customer workflows.

Do all projects need AI?

No. AI is used where it adds value. Many reliable automations are better handled with deterministic rules and integrations.

Will I need monthly subscriptions?

Custom development can be one-time, while hosting, AI APIs, third-party tools and optional maintenance may have recurring costs.

Can you work with our existing tools?

Yes. The project starts by mapping your current process and systems, then identifying what should be connected, replaced or left unchanged.
